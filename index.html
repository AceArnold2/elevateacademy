Meet the founder

Arnold, Founder of Elevate Academy

Arnold is a data analyst and software engineer specialising in machine learning, and a teacher with hands-on classroom experience in both the Cambridge and IB MYP curricula.

On the tutoring side, he started Elevate to help students reach their academic goals and discover a genuine love of learning, not just pass exams.

On the courses side, his motivation is simple: teenagers shouldn't have to wait until university to find out what they are good at. By building practical technology and career-readiness skills before graduation, students can pursue internships and jobs, choose university courses wisely, or start a career without going to university at all.

Frequently asked questions

Who are the courses for?

Our courses are designed for students aged 13 to 17. Content and pace are matched to each age group, and students can move up when they show they are ready.

What subjects does tutoring cover?

Mathematics, Science, History, Geography, Economics, Entrepreneurship, Arts, Design and the core MYP subject areas, across Cambridge, IGCSE, IB MYP, IB DP and A Level.

How long are the courses?

Our beginner courses run for four weeks. More advanced courses are longer or split into levels so nothing important is rushed.

Does my child need a computer?

For the technology courses, yes, access to a computer or laptop is needed. We share the exact requirements for each course before it starts, and we use free tools wherever we can.

Is online safety covered?

Yes. Every student completes our compulsory Online Safety and Responsible Technology Use foundation, whichever course they choose.

How does tutoring work?

We learn about your child's needs, match them with a suitable tutor, and arrange regular sessions (two a week to start). You receive a monthly progress report, and the subscription is month to month.

What does it cost?

We are finalising our prices with our first cohort. Register your interest below and we will share the details with you.

Can I cancel or get a refund?

Tutoring is month to month, so you can stop at any time. Our refund policy for courses will be published before enrollment opens.

Will my child receive a certificate?

Students who meet the attendance, participation and project requirements receive a completion certificate. It shows what they have completed, and is not a professional accreditation.
