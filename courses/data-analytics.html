Data Analytics

Learn to turn raw information into clear answers and convincing recommendations.

About this course

Data drives decisions in businesses, schools, sport and science. In this course, students learn to collect, organise, clean, analyse and present information using real-world datasets, starting with the free spreadsheet tools anyone can use.

Students learn by working with data: asking questions, finding patterns, building charts and presenting what they discover. They finish with a project that shows what they can do.

Ages13 to 17
Beginner course4 weeks
Starting levelNo experience needed
FeeShared when you register

What students learn

  1. Introduction to data

    What data is, the different types, how it is collected, why quality matters and how it informs decisions.

  2. Spreadsheets

    Using Excel or Google Sheets: entering and formatting data, sorting, filtering and basic formulas like SUM, AVERAGE, COUNT, MIN, MAX and IF.

  3. Data cleaning

    Finding missing values, removing duplicates, fixing inconsistent entries and preparing a dataset for analysis.

  4. Data analysis

    Asking questions data can answer, working out averages and percentages, comparing groups, spotting trends and avoiding misleading conclusions.

  5. Data visualisation

    Choosing the right chart, building dashboards, communicating insights and spotting misleading graphs.

  6. Introductory Python for data

    Python basics and an introduction to pandas for reading and working with tables of data.

The beginner course focuses on data literacy, spreadsheets and simple analysis. Python is taught in the advanced level so it can be covered properly rather than rushed.

What students build

  • An analysis of student survey results
  • An exploration of school attendance data
  • A sales analysis for a fictional small business
  • An investigation of sports performance statistics
  • A dashboard showing trends in a dataset, with evidence-based recommendations

Learning pathway

Students move up when they show they are ready, not just because of their age.

Beginner

Data literacy, spreadsheets and simple analysis.

Intermediate

Data cleaning, statistics, visualisation and dashboards.

Advanced

Python, pandas, larger datasets, more complex analysis and portfolio projects.

Good to know

  • Equipment: a computer or laptop with an internet connection. We use free tools such as Google Sheets.
  • Online safety: every student completes our Online Safety foundation first.
  • Certificate: students who meet the attendance, participation and project requirements receive a completion certificate. It is not a professional accreditation.
