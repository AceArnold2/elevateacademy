Technology & Digital Skills

Become a confident, independent and responsible user of the technology you rely on every day.

Enroll Register your interest

About this course

Technology is part of school, work and everyday life, but being good at using it is a skill that has to be learned. This course builds strong digital foundations: using computers well, producing polished work, researching reliably, collaborating online and using AI tools wisely.

It is a great starting point for students who want to build their confidence before, or alongside, more specialised courses like Web Development and Data Analytics.

Ages13 to 17
Beginner course4 weeks
Starting levelNo experience needed
FeeShared when you register

What students learn

  1. Computer fundamentals

    How hardware and software work, operating systems, files and folders, storage, and basic troubleshooting.

  2. Digital productivity

    Word processing, presentations, spreadsheets, exporting files, cloud storage and keeping schoolwork organised and backed up.

  3. Internet research and information literacy

    Searching effectively, judging whether a source can be trusted, spotting misinformation and referencing sources properly.

  4. Digital communication and collaboration

    Professional email, online meetings, shared documents and working well in online teams.

  5. Digital creativity

    Visual communication, basic graphic design, digital storytelling and building a digital portfolio.

  6. AI and emerging technologies

    What AI is, how to write good prompts, how to check AI answers, its limits and biases, and how to use it responsibly in schoolwork.

Students build the foundations in the beginner course. Research, collaboration, creative work and AI literacy deepen in the intermediate level.

What students build

  • A polished presentation
  • A well-formatted report
  • A documented piece of online research
  • A shared project completed with classmates
  • A digital portfolio
  • A task completed with an AI tool, with the results checked and verified

Learning pathway

Students move up when they show they are ready, not just because of their age.

Beginner

Computer use, files, productivity tools and safe internet use.

Intermediate

Research, collaboration, digital content creation and AI literacy.

Advanced

More advanced productivity workflows, digital projects and tools for each student's chosen pathway.

Good to know

  • Equipment: a computer or laptop with an internet connection. We use free tools wherever we can.
  • Online safety: every student completes our Online Safety foundation first.
  • Certificate: students who meet the attendance, participation and project requirements receive a completion certificate. It is not a professional accreditation.
